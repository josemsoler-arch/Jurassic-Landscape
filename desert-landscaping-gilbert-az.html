What is Desert Landscaping?

Desert landscaping, also known as xeriscaping, is a sustainable approach to landscape design that works with Arizona's natural desert environment rather than against it. By using native and drought-tolerant plants, decorative rock, and water-efficient irrigation, desert landscaping creates beautiful outdoor spaces that require minimal water and maintenance.

In Gilbert, AZ, where water conservation is crucial, desert landscaping isn't just environmentally responsible—it's also cost-effective and stunning. Our designs reduce water usage by up to 75% compared to traditional grass lawns while creating unique, low-maintenance landscapes.

Benefits of Desert Landscaping in Gilbert

💧 Water Savings

Reduce water usage by 50-75% with native plants and efficient irrigation. Lower utility bills and help conserve Gilbert's water resources.

🏡 Low Maintenance

Native plants thrive naturally in Gilbert's climate with minimal care. Less mowing, trimming, and fertilizing means more time to enjoy your yard.

🌵 Year-Round Beauty

Desert plants provide color and interest throughout all seasons. Many bloom spectacularly in spring and maintain beauty even in summer heat.

💰 Increased Property Value

Well-designed desert landscaping boosts curb appeal and home value. Buyers in Gilbert appreciate water-wise, low-maintenance landscapes.

🦋 Wildlife Friendly

Native plants attract hummingbirds, butterflies, and beneficial insects. Create a thriving ecosystem in your Gilbert backyard.

🌡️ Heat Resistant

Plants naturally adapted to Gilbert's extreme heat don't suffer during summer. They thrive when temperatures soar above 110°F.

Popular Desert Plants for Gilbert Landscapes

Trees

  • Palo Verde - Arizona's state tree with brilliant yellow spring blooms
  • Mesquite - Fast-growing shade tree with filtered canopy
  • Desert Willow - Beautiful flowers and graceful form
  • Ironwood - Dense shade and purple flowers

Shrubs & Accent Plants

  • Texas Sage - Purple blooms after monsoon rains
  • Red Yucca - Coral flower spikes, hummingbird favorite
  • Desert Marigold - Yellow flowers nearly year-round
  • Fairy Duster - Red pom-pom blooms, compact size
  • Brittlebush - Yellow daisies in spring, silvery foliage

Cacti & Succulents

  • Saguaro - Iconic Arizona cactus (transplants available)
  • Golden Barrel Cactus - Spherical shape, low maintenance
  • Agave - Dramatic sculptural forms in many varieties
  • Prickly Pear - Colorful pads and edible fruit
  • Aloe Vera - Medicinal properties, orange blooms
