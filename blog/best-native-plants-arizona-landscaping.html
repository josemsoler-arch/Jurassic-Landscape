10 Best Native Plants for Arizona Landscaping

Native Arizona plants are perfectly adapted to our desert climate, requiring minimal water and maintenance while providing stunning year-round beauty. Here are our top 10 picks for Gilbert, Queen Creek, and Mesa landscapes.

1. Palo Verde (Parkinsonia species)

Why We Love It: Arizona's state tree provides brilliant yellow blooms in spring that cover the entire tree. Fast-growing with filtered shade perfect for desert landscapes.

Water Needs: Low once established

Mature Size: 25-30 feet tall and wide

Best For: Specimen tree, providing shade without blocking views

2. Texas Sage / Texas Ranger (Leucophyllum frutescens)

Why We Love It: Purple tubular blooms appear after monsoon rains, creating waves of color. Silver-gray foliage provides year-round interest.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: 4-6 feet tall and wide

Best For: Hedges, foundation plantings, mass plantings

3. Red Yucca (Hesperaloe parviflora)

Why We Love It: Coral-pink flower spikes bloom from spring through fall, attracting hummingbirds. Grass-like foliage adds texture contrast.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: 2-3 feet tall, 3-4 feet wide

Best For: Accent plant, borders, wildlife gardens

4. Desert Marigold (Baileya multiradiata)

Why We Love It: Cheerful yellow daisy-like flowers bloom nearly year-round in Arizona. Self-seeds readily for natural spread.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: 12-18 inches tall and wide

Best For: Ground cover, wildflower meadows, naturalized areas

5. Fairy Duster (Calliandra eriophylla)

Why We Love It: Delicate red pom-pom flowers appear in spring. Compact size makes it perfect for smaller spaces.

Water Needs: Low

Mature Size: 2-3 feet tall and wide

Best For: Rock gardens, front yards, container gardens

6. Desert Willow (Chilopsis linearis)

Why We Love It: Fragrant trumpet-shaped flowers in pink, purple, or white bloom spring through fall. Attracts hummingbirds and bees.

Water Needs: Low to moderate

Mature Size: 15-25 feet tall, 10-20 feet wide

Best For: Small to medium shade tree, wildlife gardens

7. Brittlebush (Encelia farinosa)

Why We Love It: Masses of bright yellow daisy flowers in spring. Silver-gray foliage provides contrast year-round.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: 2-3 feet tall and wide

Best For: Mass plantings, slopes, naturalized areas

8. Golden Barrel Cactus (Echinocactus grusonii)

Why We Love It: Perfect spherical shape with golden spines creates dramatic focal points. Extremely low maintenance.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: 2-3 feet diameter

Best For: Accent plant, rock gardens, modern landscapes

9. Agave (Various species)

Why We Love It: Architectural sculptural forms in blue, gray, or variegated varieties. Dramatic focal points that require virtually no care.

Water Needs: Very low

Mature Size: Varies by species, 1-6 feet

Best For: Contemporary landscapes, containers, accent plantings

10. Penstemon (Penstemon species)

Why We Love It: Tubular flowers in red, pink, purple, or blue attract hummingbirds. Multiple species native to Arizona.

Water Needs: Low to moderate

Mature Size: 1-4 feet depending on species

Best For: Perennial gardens, hummingbird gardens, cottage-style landscapes

Planting Tips for Success

  • Plant in fall or spring: Avoid summer planting when heat stress is highest
  • Water regularly during establishment: Even drought-tolerant plants need water the first year
  • Use proper soil: Ensure excellent drainage to prevent root rot
  • Give them space: Native plants grow larger than you think—don't crowd them
  • Buy from local nurseries: Plants acclimated to Arizona conditions perform better
